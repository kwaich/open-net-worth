# Something bought in installments

A home, car or anything else bought IN INSTALLMENTS (an off-plan apartment, a car on a payment plan): what they have already paid is theirs and must count toward net worth, so never record only a future commitment.

- Add the thing as an asset at its full contract price.
- Add a loan account for the unpaid balance and link it to the asset, so net worth shows exactly what they have paid so far.
- If the payments have dates, offer to add the schedule as a commitment so the upcoming installments show in their plan.
